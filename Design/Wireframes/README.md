## Wireframes

Low-fidelity mobile wireframes for BatchBite (iPhone 16 Pro Max, 440 x 956).

**Figma:** [BatchBite Wireframes](https://www.figma.com/design/K5oHxJBAc1sgCpitmwJCIG/BatchBite-Wireframes)

### Screens

| # | Screen | User | Purpose |
|---|--------|------|---------|
| 1 | Login | All | Sign in or go to sign up |
| 2 | Customer Home | Customer | Search and browse available batches |
| 3 | Product Details | Customer | View item, pick variant and quantity, add to cart |
| 4 | Cart & Checkout | Customer | Review cart, choose pickup time, confirm order |
| 5 | My Orders | Customer | Track order status and pickup time |
| 6 | Owner Dashboard | Business Owner | See active orders and open main tools |
| 7 | Create Production Batch | Business Owner | Set product, deadline, max quantity and pickup date |
| 8 | Kitchen Orders | Kitchen Staff | View today's batch orders and update status |
